Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Trail Notes is a personal field-notes app for mapping trails. This policy explains what it does with your information.

What stays on your device

Your trail notes, answers, recordings and the speech-to-text model are stored on your own phone or computer, inside the app. Turning a recording into text happens on your device. Recordings are not sent to us or to any speech service.

What goes to Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, Trail Notes saves your completed trail notes as files in your own Google Drive and reads your trail-list Google Sheet that you link. It uses the Google permissions you approve on the sign-in screen, only to do that. Trail Notes does not copy that data to any server of ours, because there isn't one.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than running the features above. We don't use analytics or tracking.

Google user data

Trail Notes' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Delete a trail or area inside the app to remove it from your device. Delete the Trail Notes files from your Google Drive to remove them there. You can revoke the app's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
